Tech Repairs

Documenting hardware repair logs, console modifications, and retro electronics restoration work.

Game Boy Advance SP - IPS Backlight Screen & Shell Modification

A step-by-step restoration of a heavily worn AGS-001 Game Boy Advance SP. Includes replacing the front-lit screen with a modern, high-contrast IPS LCD panel, installing a custom transparent shell, and upgrading the battery with a custom high-capacity LiPo cell.

MacBook Pro 15-inch - Thermal Repaste & Internal Dusting

Diagnosing overheating and fan throttling issues on a legacy MacBook Pro. Walkthrough of removing old dried-out thermal paste, cleaning dual blower fans, applying Arctic MX-4 thermal compound, and measuring temperature drops under load.

Custom Mechanical Keyboard - Desoldering & Lubing Gateron Switches

Desoldering factory switches from a hot-swap-incompatible mechanical keyboard, cleaning the PCB contacts, lubricating Gateron Brown switches with Krytox 205g0, and soldering them back to achieve a quieter, premium tactile feel.
