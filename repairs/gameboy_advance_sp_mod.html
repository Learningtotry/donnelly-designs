Game Boy Advance SP Mod

Andrew Donnelly

Installing an IPS backlit screen and custom transparent shell replacement on a retro console. This upgrade makes a world of difference for gameplay in low-light conditions.

The original AGS-001 screen is front-lit, which means it relies on reflecting light from a front diffuser panel. In contrast, modern IPS screens are backlit, offering vibrant colors, incredible viewing angles, and adjustable brightness levels. For this project, I chose a drop-in IPS kit and a matching transparent purple replacement housing.
