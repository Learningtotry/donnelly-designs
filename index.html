Research

I've been in multiple labs in my time at UC Davis. I was first in Prof. Hong's lab, working as a data analyst for his PhD student Hudson Shih. There I used Numpy to analyze twisted silicon bilayers in order to characterize the morphology as the lattice was annealed. I created a custom algorithm in Numpy that allowed me to quantify the overall convexity of the wells that formed which showed conclusively that the structures were becoming more elongated.

I have been part of Prof. Yang's lab in the Department of Engineering since approximately March. Here, I've done layout for a high-speed digital 4-layer PCB that we're using for some research. This design integrates a Sony MT9P031 image sensor with surrounding circuitry for power and serialization. I reduced track widths, used differential pairs to support SI, and generally kept things up to code.
