'3 Reasons to be Proud to be an American': Reason 1 (Japanese edition)

I decided that, for the sheer fun of it, I would translate an old YAF pamphlet about why you should be proud of being an American into Japanese. It helps me learn about political terms and the pamphlet itself has a lot of common expressions which helps me familiarize myself with sort of casual English->Japanese translation. This is the first part and is unbelievably rough right now, but I'm putting it out to force myself to work on it, anyway. I'll take it down if YAF has a problem with it, but I already have permission from the Dr. Folsom to translate it.
